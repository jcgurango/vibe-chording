Rules

A chord may follow another if it satisfies any of:

Click a chord to try it at the insertion point, double-click (or Enter) to add it. Solid arrows come out of the chord before, dashed arrows lead into the chord after; red adds tension, green resolves it.
Click a piano key to keep only chords with that note; shift-click to drop chords that have it.

Progression

    Nothing yet. Click a chord on the map to hear it voice-led, double-click to add it. Every chord here is voice-led: root and 5th in the left hand, three voices above with the 3rd and 7th always present. The lines between chords are insertion points; use / to pin the bass and ▲ to pin the top note.
