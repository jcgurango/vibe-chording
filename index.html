Vibe Chording

Rules

A chord may follow another if it satisfies any of:

Click a chord to hear it pure and add it. Lines are named moves; bright arrows are what can follow the last chord.
Click a piano key to keep only chords with that note; shift-click to drop chords that have it.

Progression

    Nothing yet. Pick a chord on the map to start. Clicking a chord here plays it in its voice-led form: root and 5th in the left hand, three voices above with the 3rd and 7th always present. Use / to pin the bass to another note.
