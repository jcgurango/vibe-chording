Skeleton and Moves

What am I looking at?
  • Columns follow the chain of fourths: brighter to the left, darker to the right, home in the middle. Rows are the palettes from the catalogue.
  • The sequence is open-ended. The blue → ? in the bar is the usual focus: where could the last chord lead? Every skeletal chord is scored for that step; brightness and the small bar show the score, three dots the tension level.
  • Blue arrows leave the last chord for the strongest next chords, with pills on the way: paths by way of a dominant, a ii–V, a slide, and so on. Hover a chord to see all of its moves; click a pill to add the move and the chord it lands on.
  • Click a gap (the arrow between two cards, or a path in the space) to work between two chords: moves into the right chord sit along the path, and the best chords that could sit between show their way in and out. Wrapping back to the first chord is one of the gaps, not a rule.
  • Click adds and sounds a chord with the voice leading intact. Shift-click only sounds it. Click a card to hear it again. Backspace removes the last chord, Esc returns to the end.
  • Drag or scroll to pan, pinch or ctrl-scroll to zoom.
